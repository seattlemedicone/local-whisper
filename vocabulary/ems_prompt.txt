EMS clinical dictation for Seattle Medic One paramedics transporting patients to Harborview Medical Center or UW Medical Center Montlake. Dictation commands: new paragraph, new line, comma, period, colon, semicolon, question mark. The ETA is 14 colon 45. The patient is diaphoretic with cholelithiasis, chest pain, or low back pain. STEMI care: a 12-lead ECG may show ST elevation and reciprocal ST depression; activate the cath lab for PCI. Trauma care: tourniquet, pelvic binder, needle thoracostomy, tension pneumothorax, and tranexamic acid. Stroke care: last known well, FAST-ED, large vessel occlusion, and thrombectomy. Overdose care: naloxone, Narcan, respiratory depression, and polysubstance toxidrome. Medications include aspirin, nitroglycerin, fentanyl, epinephrine, norepinephrine, amiodarone, adenosine, atropine, midazolam, ketamine, ondansetron, albuterol, dextrose, and sodium bicarbonate. Airway and monitoring terms include supraglottic airway, i-gel, endotracheal, cricothyrotomy, intraosseous, room air, nasal cannula, non-rebreather face mask, NRFM, CPAP, BVM, SpO2, EtCO2, and mm Hg.
